Catch-up & personalised learning

One-to-one sessions, planned around your child.

Every child learns differently. Some need a little more practice, others need help understanding a particular subject or topic. I work with each learner at their own pace, using activities and resources that make learning easier to understand.

All packages are one-to-one. The difference between them is the level of planning, assessment and follow-up your child receives.

Choose the level of support that works for your child

Tier 1 — Essential
Building Strong Foundations
For children who understand most of their schoolwork but need more time to practise and feel confident.
Includes
  • Two 90-minute sessions per week.
  • A short check-in during the first session.
  • A plan covering the topics your child needs to practise.
  • Practice sheets and activities.
  • A short update after every session.
KSh 16,000 per month
Tier 2 — Personalised
Focused Subject Support
For children who find certain subjects, topics or skills challenging and need a more focused approach.
Includes
  • Three 90-minute sessions per week.
  • A 30-minute initial assessment.
  • Clear learning goals based on your child's needs.
  • Activities prepared specifically for your child.
  • An update every two weeks.
  • A monthly progress report.
KSh 28,500 per month
Tier 3 — Comprehensive
An Individual Learning Programme
For children who need regular, structured support across several learning areas or need a more personalised programme.
Includes
  • Four 90-minute sessions per week.
  • An initial assessment to understand your child's learning needs.
  • An individual learning plan.
  • A personalised pack of learning resources.
  • A weekly progress update.
  • A monthly meeting with the parent.
  • A report at the end of the programme.
KSh 45,000 per month
Please note: These sessions are designed to help children practise, understand and build confidence. They do not replace a specialist assessment. If I notice something that may need further attention, I will discuss it with you and recommend an appropriate professional.

Getting started is easy

You don't need to go through a long process. Here's how we get started:

01.
Get in touch
Send me a WhatsApp message. We will arrange a free 20-minute call to talk about your child and what you are looking for.
02.
Choose a package
We will agree on the package, number of sessions, timetable and location before getting started. I'll confirm everything in writing.
03.
Complete the learner form
I'll ask you to share a few details about your child, including the relevant contacts, permissions and confirmation that a parent or responsible adult will be at home.
04.
Make payment and begin
Payment is made through M-Pesa before the first session. You'll receive a receipt for every payment.

A few things to know before booking

I want us to have a clear and comfortable arrangement from the beginning. Here are the booking and payment guidelines.

Payments and package duration
  • Payment is made in advance through M-Pesa to the Till number provided.
  • A receipt is issued for every payment.
  • Monthly packages are valid for five weeks from the first session.
Changing or missing a session
  • More than 24 hours' notice: You can reschedule without an extra charge.
  • Less than 24 hours' notice: 50% of the session fee is charged.
  • Missed session without notice: The full session fee is charged.
  • If I need to cancel: You will receive a replacement session or credit.
  • If your child is unwell: Send me a message that morning and we can reschedule without a charge.
Public holidays and additional hours
  • Sessions falling on public holidays will be moved to another day within your package period.
  • Need more time than your package allows? We can agree on extra hours beforehand. These will be charged at your package rate.
Ending a package
If you decide to stop, please give two weeks' notice. Any prepaid weeks that have not been used will be refunded.
What's included in the price?
Included
  • Preparation and planning for sessions.
  • Printed practice materials.
  • WhatsApp communication for scheduling.
  • Progress updates and reports included in your chosen package.
Not included
  • Textbooks.
  • Transport outside Zone A.
  • Examination fees.
  • Specialist assessments.
  • Sole responsibility for childcare.
Parent consultations are included where specified in your package. Additional consultations are charged at KSh 1,000 per 30 minutes.
Your child's safety and privacy
Your child's wellbeing matters. Sessions take place in a shared space at home, with a parent or responsible adult present.
I will not take or share photographs of your child without your written permission.

Questions parents often ask

Will you guarantee better grades?

I can't promise particular grades or results. What I can promise is thoughtful preparation, consistent sessions and honest feedback about your child's progress.

Does the monthly package mean unlimited sessions?

No. Each package comes with a set number of sessions and hours. You'll know exactly what is included before booking.

Can you work with a child who is struggling with learning?

Yes. I provide patient, structured and individualised sessions to help children work through topics they find difficult. I am not a specialist assessor, so if a child needs a professional assessment, I will let you know.

Is supervised play the same as babysitting?

No. These sessions focus on play, reading and age-appropriate enrichment activities. A parent or trusted adult must remain at home throughout.

How does holiday tuition work?

Holiday sessions take place on weekday mornings during school holidays. You can choose between:

  • Holiday Light: Three mornings, two hours each morning.
  • Holiday Focus: Five mornings, three hours each morning.

On the first day, we'll identify two or three things to work on. At the end of the programme, you'll receive a short summary of how your child got on.

Spaces are limited, so it's best to book before the holidays begin.
